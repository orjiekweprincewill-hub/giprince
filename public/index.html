Meet the Founder & CEO

The vision behind Yenllet — Nigeria's fastest-growing VTU platform.

Orjiekwe Princewill Chigozie

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Born: July 6, 2008

Driven by a passion for technology and a vision to simplify digital payments in Nigeria, I founded Yenllet — a platform built to make airtime, data, electricity, cable TV, betting wallet funding, and education pins accessible and affordable for every Nigerian. Growing up in a country where reliable, fast, and affordable bill payment services are still a luxury for many, I wanted to build a solution that puts power back in the hands of users. Yenllet combines the cheapest VTU rates, instant transaction processing, secure wallet funding via Flutterwave, and a cashback reward system that gives back to our loyal users. My mission is to make Yenllet the most trusted VTU platform in Nigeria — one transaction at a time.

Yenllet is a Gustmii company — part of the Gustmii ecosystem that also includes Gustmii Marketplace (Nigeria's premier 3D social commerce platform).
