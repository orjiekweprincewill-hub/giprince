Vendor Withdrawals
How Vendor Withdrawals Work
Vendors earn commission on every purchase made by users they refer. When they request a withdrawal, the amount is NOT sent to their bank account (to avoid CBN issues). Instead, when you approve, the amount is credited to their Yenllet wallet instantly — which they can then spend on airtime, data, bills, or transfer to other Yenllet users.

Gift Banners
How Gift Banners Work
Upload a promotional banner (e.g. "Free ₦500 cashback!"). Users see it on their dashboard. When they tap Claim, the cashback amount is credited to their cashback balance. Use Max Claims to cap the giveaway. Use Restricted Service to require the user to have used a specific service (e.g. data) before they can claim. Each user can only claim once per banner — duplicate claims are blocked by the database.
