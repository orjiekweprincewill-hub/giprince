Orphaned Deposits (Trapped Funds)
What are orphaned deposits?
When admin deletes a user's bank account (because the BVN was fake), the old Flutterwave virtual account still exists on Flutterwave's side. If anyone — the banned user themselves, or someone who still has the old account number saved in their bank app — sends money to it, Yenllet's webhook receives the deposit but cannot credit anyone (because no user owns that account_number anymore).

Instead of letting those funds disappear silently, Yenllet logs each one here. Review each deposit and decide what to do manually: refund the sender via bank transfer, keep the funds in escrow, or contact Flutterwave support to reverse the transaction.

Instead, when you approve, the amount is credited to their Yenllet wallet instantly — which they can then spend on airtime, data, bills, or transfer to other Yenllet users.

Promo Cards
How Promo Cards Work
Upload a promotional image (e.g. "MTN 1GB for ₦480"), select the service (Data, TV, Electricity, Education, Bet, Airtime), pick the provider (e.g. MTN) and the specific plan (e.g. 1GB - Monthly).

The card appears on the user dashboard in the same row as the cashback pill (top of the home screen). When a user taps the card, the matching purchase modal opens instantly with the provider pre-selected — so they complete the purchase in 1-2 taps.

Difference from Gift Banners: Gift banners give free cashback when claimed. Promo cards are deep-link banners — they don't give anything free, they just showcase a specific plan and let users jump straight to buying it.

API Health Check
How this works
Yenllet depends on 4 external APIs: ClubKonnect (airtime/data/TV/electricity), Flutterwave (payments + virtual accounts), CheckMyNinBvn (BVN/NIN verification), and Resend (transactional emails).

This page checks each one live (click Re-check to refresh) and shows: configuration status, latency (response time), and any errors. Use this BEFORE users complain — if a service is down, you'll know instantly.

Note: ClubKonnect and CheckMyNinBvn are pinged with a lightweight request (free). Flutterwave is NOT pinged (to avoid charges) — only its env var is checked. Resend is checked by verifying the env var exists.
